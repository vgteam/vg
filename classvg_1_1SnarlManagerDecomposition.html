Detailed Description

A view of the snarls and chains in a SnarlManager, through the SnarlDecomposition interface.

A chain alternates chain nodes and snarls, starting and ending with a chain node. Each chain node has a position, counting from 0 at the start bound of the chain. The snarl of rank i lies between the chain nodes at positions i and i + 1.

Each snarl is oriented forward along its chain. So a Snarl that is reversed in parent starts at its end() flipped and ends at its start() flipped.

In a looping chain, the last snarl leads back into the first chain node. That node is both bounds of the chain, and one child at position 0.

Each node that is not a chain node is a free node. It is a trivial chain whose start-to-end traversal visits the node forward.

The view keeps a reference to the manager, which must outlive it and must not change. It does not keep the graph. A graph passed to a method must have the same nodes and edges as the graph the view was made with.

Note
Unlike a SnarlDistanceIndex, the view puts top-level chains directly in the root, and shows snarls that have no children.

◆ get_parent()

net_handle_t vg::SnarlManagerDecomposition::get_parent ( const net_handle_t &  child) const
overridevirtual

Get the parent snarl of a chain, or the parent chain of a snarl or node. If the child is start-to-end or end-to-start, and the parent is a chain, the chain comes out facing the same way, accounting for the relative orientation of the child snarl or node in the chain. Otherwise, everything is produced as start-to-end, even if that is not actually a realizable traversal of a snarl or chain. May not be called on the root snarl.

Also works on snarl boundary sentinels.

Implements handlegraph::SnarlDecomposition.

Find the snarl or chain, and its endpoint, where a traversal of it can start or end at a net graph element or sentinel.

Parameters
netOne of:
  • A sentinel facing into its snarl, if is_start, or out of it otherwise. This gives the snarl and the bound of the sentinel.
