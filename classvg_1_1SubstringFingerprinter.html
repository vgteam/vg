Detailed Description

A precomputed table for quickly computing the fingerprint of any substring s of length n, up to a maximum length.

The fingerprint is the polynomial s[0] * B^(n-1) + ... + s[n-1] modulo the prime 2^61 - 1, for a fixed base B. Two different strings of length at most n have the same fingerprint with probability at most n / 2^61.
