Detailed Description

A surveyor that finds the realizable traversals of one snarl or chain at a time, by walking the graph.

A walk crosses each child as the survey says the traversals of that child allow. So the surveyor must survey each child before its parent.

Find where walks along a chain from some chain nodes can end.

Each start is the position of a chain node, and true to leave it going in reverse along the chain. Here the end bound has position chain.size() even in a looping chain. A walk that comes to a bound of a looping chain can leave there, and can also go on around the loop, since its boundary node is both bounds.

Precondition
Each snarl in the chain has been surveyed.
