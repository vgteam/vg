Detailed Description

Content-aware node chopping to enable sequence deduplication in GBZ.

Full unclipped graphs contain long unaligned segments such as centromeres that are repetitive and hence compressible. GBZ used to store a naive concatenation of the sequences in memory. From version 4, it can store an arbitrary superstring that can be potentially much shorter.

Here we use Content-Defined Chunking (CDC) to identify good positions for chopping long nodes. We simultaneously collect a list of superstrings that cover the sequence labels of all chopped pieces.
