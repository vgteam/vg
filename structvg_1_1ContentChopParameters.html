◆ anchor_radius

size_t vg::ContentChopParameters::anchor_radius = 32

A position is a candidate for chopping, if the forward strand k-mer starting at it has a smaller hash than any k-mer starting within this radius. Serves as a strict lower bound for the distance between candidate positions. Similar to minimizers with window length 2 * anchor_radius + 1.

◆ kmer_length

◆ max_node_length

size_t vg::ContentChopParameters::max_node_length = 1024

Maximum allowed node length. Only nodes longer than this are chopped, and individual pieces will each be at most this length.

◆ piece_weight

double vg::ContentChopParameters::piece_weight = 16.0

Relative cost (in bp) of each chopped piece. We try to minimize L + piece_weight * N, where L is the total length of stored strings and N is the number of chopped pieces.
